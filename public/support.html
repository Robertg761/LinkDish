Support

Last updated: July 10, 2026

LinkDish is designed to keep recipe extraction simple and dependable for supported recipe pages, article-style recipes, YouTube recipe links with enough recipe detail, and user-submitted recipe photos. If something is not working as expected, the fastest first checks are:

  • Make sure the recipe link is public and still available.
  • Try pasting the link again if the source was temporarily unreachable.
  • Try a different recipe source if the site blocks extraction or is unsupported.
  • For recipe scans, use a clear, well-lit image where the ingredients and steps are readable.

Saved recipes

Free includes up to 15 personal saved recipes, and paid plans include unlimited personal saves. Personal saved recipes are stored on your device. Recipes you add to a shared Family recipe book are stored by LinkDish so household members can view them. If a saved recipe looks outdated or malformed after an app update, removing it and extracting it again should refresh the content.

Accounts and Family households

No account is required for the first 3 free recipe imports. Sign in is required to buy or restore a paid plan, create, join, or manage LinkDish Family household sharing, or add recipes to a shared Family recipe book. Depending on the active rollout mode, sign-in may use an email code or Google/Apple sign-in.

Plans and purchases

Free includes 3 total recipe imports and up to 15 saved recipes. LinkDish Plus includes 100 monthly recipe imports and unlimited saved recipes. LinkDish Family includes 250 monthly recipe imports, unlimited saved recipes, and household sharing. Mobile purchases use Apple App Store or Google Play through RevenueCat. Web subscriptions and the one-time Founding offer use RevenueCat Web Billing. If paid access is not showing, use Restore purchases or Manage billing from the Plans screen.

Privacy

For details about what information the app handles, read the LinkDish Privacy Policy.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, email support@linkdish.ca. If you are testing a pre-release build, you can also use the same channel where you received the build to report issues.

Data deletion requests

If you created a LinkDish account, you can delete it from Account in the app. Deleting the account removes server-side account, household, and shared Family recipe records that belong to the account, but App Store, Google Play, and web subscriptions must still be managed with the original billing provider. If you cannot access the app, send your request to support@linkdish.ca.
