# What's new in LinkDish

A ground-up refresh of the web app, a faster importer, and new ways to plan and
shop. Everything below ships in this release; copy here can be reused for store
listings, the changelog page and social posts.

## A cookbook you'll want to open

- **Photo-first Cookbook.** Recipes show as photo cards in a grid or a compact
  list, with shelves for "Cook again" and "Quick weeknights".
- **Find anything fast.** Search ranks titles, tags, ingredients, notes and
  steps, and highlights what matched. Filter by favorites, quick recipes (30
  minutes or less), not-cooked-yet, collections and tags.
- **Organize your way.** Heart your favorites, add tags (with suggestions), and
  group recipes into collections with their own emoji.
- **Dark mode.** Follows your device, or pick Light or Dark in Settings.
- **Undo instead of "Are you sure?"** Deleting a recipe or a shopping item can
  be undone from the toast.

## Recipes that adapt to you

- **Scale by servings.** "Serves 4 → 6" updates every amount with clean
  fractions (⅔ cup, never 0.6666 cup), and counts agree ("1 large egg").
- **US or metric, for real.** Switch between the recipe's original units, US and
  metric. Oven temperatures convert too, and baking staples convert between
  cups and grams (marked as approximate).
- **Tap to check off ingredients** as you go; the ticks carry into cook mode.
- **Ratings and a cook log.** "Cooked 3× · last 2 weeks ago."
- **Better editor.** Edit title, description, servings, times, ingredients
  (with sections), steps and notes, with a guard against losing changes.

## Cook mode, calmer

- **Pick up where you left off.** Cook mode remembers your step, checked
  ingredients and timers.
- **Timers that keep running.** Start timers straight from a step; they stay in
  a dock at the bottom of the screen even if you leave cook mode, with pause,
  +1 minute, a chime and a notification when they finish.
- **Big, readable steps** with adjustable text size, "For this step" ingredients,
  a side-by-side layout on tablets and desktops, keep-awake and read-aloud.
- **Finish strong.** Rate the recipe, log the cook with a note, and send the
  ingredients to your shopping list.

## Save from anywhere

- **Links from recipe sites and blogs**, YouTube (including Shorts), Pinterest
  pins (LinkDish follows the pin to the original recipe) and TikTok captions.
- **Paste recipe text** from a caption, a message or a note.
- **Snap photos** of a cookbook page or a handwritten card (up to 4 per recipe).
  Photos are resized on your device before upload.
- **Paste a stack of links** at once, or share while offline: LinkDish queues
  them and imports them when you're back online.
- **Already saved?** LinkDish tells you before spending an import.
- **Faster repeat imports.** Popular recipes other cooks already imported come
  back almost instantly.
- **Clear progress and honest errors.** See each stage, cancel anytime, and get
  a specific next step (try again, paste the text, scan a photo) when a page
  won't cooperate.

## Plan the week, shop in one trip

- **Meal planner.** Drop recipes or notes ("Leftovers", "Eat out") onto the
  days of the week, set servings per meal, and move meals around.
- **One tap from plan to list.** Turn the week's plan into a shopping list with
  quantities merged across recipes.
- **Shopping list by aisle.** Items group by store section (produce, dairy,
  pantry and so on) or by recipe, with clean amounts, recipe labels, a "Buy
  again" row, multi-line paste, share or print, and an option to hide pantry
  staples.
- **Family households** share the cookbook and keep the shopping list in sync.

## Your recipes, safe and portable

- **Download a backup** of your whole cookbook, including favorites, tags,
  ratings, notes, collections and your meal plan (scanned photos optional).
- **Switch in a minute.** Import from Paprika and Mela exports, or recipe data
  (JSON-LD) files. Duplicates are skipped for you.
- **Export as text** (Markdown) for a human-readable copy.
- **Protect my recipes** asks your browser not to clear LinkDish's storage.

## Faster everywhere

- The web app no longer downloads a 446 KB icon font before it can draw icons,
  and sign-in code only loads when you need it. The first load of the Cookbook
  needs about 140 KB of compressed JavaScript.
- Pages load on demand, the app updates itself with a "fresh version ready"
  prompt, and it keeps working offline.
- **Keyboard friendly.** ⌘K / Ctrl K opens a command palette for recipes, pages
  and actions; press ? for all shortcuts.

## On Android

- A smoother, faster Cookbook with favorites and a "Quickest" sort.
- The shopping list groups by aisle, merges amounts cleanly and has "Clear
  checked".
- Original / US / Metric units with oven temperature conversion.
- Many fixes, including shared links to recipes you already saved opening the
  saved copy instead of spending an import.
