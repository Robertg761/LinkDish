Marry Me Bacon Chicken Gnocchi 

1. To a large skillet add 12 ounces of bacon chopped at a low to medium heat. Cook until crispy. Remove from pan leaving the bacon grease.
2. While bacon cooks take 2 chicken breasts and cut them horizontally making 4 thin ones. Season however you’d like. Then to a separate bowl mix 1/2 cup of grated Parmesan with a 1/2 cup of Italian style bread crumbs. Press the chicken in the bread crumbs on both sides.
3. Take the chicken and add that to your skillet. Cook 3-4 minutes then flip and cook another 3-4 minutes. Cook at a medium to high heat. Then remove the chicken from the pan.
4. Wipe out any burnt bits from your pan. Now add around a tablespoon of sun dried tomato oil and about 1/4 cup of chopped sun dried tomatoes to your pan. Along with 1 package of gnocchi. Toss around until lightly toasted. Then add 1 tablespoon of minced garlic and cook until fragrant.
5. Now to that pan add 3/4 cups of low sodium chicken broth and 1 cup of heavy cream. Season well with smoked paprika, pepper and Italian seasoning. Stir until combined. Now add 1 cup of freshly shredded Parmesan cheese. Stir until smooth.
6. Add 1-2 cups of chopped fresh spinach. Stir for a couple of minutes.
7. Add half of your bacon back to the pan and mix then top with your chicken sliced up, more shredded Parmesan and the rest of your bacon. 
8. Enjoy!!
