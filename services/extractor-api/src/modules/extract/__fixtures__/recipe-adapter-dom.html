Crispy Smashed Potatoes

Yield: 4 to 6 servings. Prep time: 10 minutes. Cook time: 1 hour 5 minutes.

  • 2 pounds small Yukon Gold potatoes
  • 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • Kosher salt, to taste
  • 2 pounds small Yukon Gold potatoes
  1. Boil potatoes in salted water until tender, about 20 minutes.
  2. Drain, then smash each potato flat with the bottom of a measuring cup.
  3. Roast with olive oil at 450F until deeply browned and crisp, 40 to 45 minutes.
Calories 210 Protein 3g Carbs 28g Fat 10g Fiber 3g Sodium 320mg

Notes

Leftovers keep for 3 days.
