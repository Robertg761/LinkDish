My Grandmother's Tomato Soup

Every winter my grandmother made this soup on Sundays. It takes about an hour and feeds a crowd of 6 people with bread on the side.

Prep time: 15 minutes

Cook time: 45 minutes

What you need

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cans (28 oz) whole tomatoes
  • 2 cups vegetable stock
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream

How to make it

Directions

Melt the butter in a large pot and cook the onion until soft, about 8 minutes.

Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute, then add the tomatoes and stock.

Simmer for 30 minutes, blend until smooth, and stir in the cream.

Nutrition

Calories: 190 Protein: 4 g Carbohydrates: 18 g Fat: 11 g Sugar: 9 g Sodium: 640 mg
