My Cozy Weeknight Soup

This simple pantry soup comes together quickly and feeds four people.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 4 cups vegetable stock
  • 1 can white beans

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil and cook the onion until soft.
  2. Add the stock and beans, then simmer for 20 minutes.
  3. Season to taste and serve warm.

Prep time: 10 min. Cook time: 20 min. Serves 4 people.

Nutrition per serving: Calories 320, Protein 14 g, Carbohydrates 28 g, Fat 9 g, Fiber 8 g, Sugar 4 g, Sodium 540 mg.
