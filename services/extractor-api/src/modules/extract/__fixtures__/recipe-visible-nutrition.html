Skillet White Beans

Ingredients

  • 2 cans white beans
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon

Instructions

  1. Warm the oil and add the beans.
  2. Cook until the beans are silky and hot.
  3. Finish with lemon juice and serve.

Prep time: 5 min. Cook time: 12 min. Yield: 4 servings.

Nutrition

Calories 280 Protein 11 g Carbohydrates 31 g Fat 10 g Fiber 9 g Sugar 2 g Sodium 480 mg
