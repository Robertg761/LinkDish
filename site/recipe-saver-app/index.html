Why a recipe saver

A bookmark remembers.
A cookbook helps.

Browser bookmarks and screenshots remember where a recipe lives. LinkDish turns it into something you can search, scale, plan with and cook from.

  • Save from anywhere

    Recipe sites, YouTube and Shorts descriptions, TikTok captions, Pinterest pins, photos of cookbook pages and pasted text all land in the same cookbook.

  • Find it again

    Mark favorites, group recipes into collections, add tags, and search titles, ingredients and notes with the best match first.

  • Cook from it

    Large, focused steps and timers taken from the method. Timers keep running between steps, and cook mode remembers where you were.

  • Make it fit

    Scale servings and switch between US and metric, oven temperatures included, without doing the math yourself.

  • Plan and shop

    Put recipes on the days you'll cook them and get one shopping list, grouped by aisle, for the whole week.

  • Keep it yours

    Every recipe keeps its source credited. Export a full backup whenever you like, or bring recipes over from a Paprika export.

Questions

Recipe saver
questions.

Is the LinkDish recipe saver free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 3 recipe imports and up to 15 saved recipes, with cook mode, the shopping list and offline access. No card is required. Plus adds 100 imports a month and unlimited saves.

Where does LinkDish work?

On Android and as a web app in any modern browser. On iPhone and iPad, open the web app in Safari and add it to your Home Screen. A native iPhone app is on the way.

Does LinkDish remove the original source?

No. Every imported recipe keeps its original source visible and one tap away.

Can I use my saved recipes offline?

Yes. Saved recipes open without a connection. Importing a new recipe needs the internet.

Can I get my recipes out again?

Yes. You can export a full backup of your cookbook at any time.
