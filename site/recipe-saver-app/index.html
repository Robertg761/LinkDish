How it works

From recipe link to dinner in three steps.

1

Paste or share

Send LinkDish a public recipe page from your browser or paste its link into the app.

2

Save the useful parts

Ingredients, steps, timing, servings, the photo, and the original source become one readable entry.

3

Cook and shop

Scale the recipe, use cook mode, start parsed timers, and move ingredients to your shopping list.

Frequently asked

Recipe saver questions.

Can I use LinkDish for free?

Yes. The free plan includes three recipe imports and space for up to fifteen saved recipes. No card is required.

Where does LinkDish work?

LinkDish is available on Android and the web. On iPhone and iPad, the web app can be added to your Home Screen from Safari.

Does LinkDish remove the original source?

No. Every imported recipe keeps its original source visible and one tap away.
