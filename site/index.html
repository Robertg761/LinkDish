Save from anywhere

Found it somewhere?
Save it here.

Paste a link, share from another app, snap a photo or paste the text. LinkDish pulls out the ingredients, steps, times and photo, and keeps the original source one tap away.

  • Recipe websites

    Paste or share any public recipe page, from big food sites to small blogs. You get the recipe without the pop-ups and the life story.

  • YouTube & Shorts

    Share a video or a Short. When the recipe is in the description, it becomes ingredients and steps, with the video one tap away.

  • TikTok captions

    Share a TikTok whose caption spells out the recipe, and LinkDish turns that caption into a proper recipe card.

  • Pinterest pins

    Share a pin and LinkDish follows it to the recipe page it links to, then saves that recipe with its real source.

  • Photos & cookbook scans

    Snap a cookbook page, a handwritten recipe card or a screenshot. LinkDish reads it into a recipe you can edit.

  • Pasted text

    Recipe stuck in an email, a note or a message? Paste the text and LinkDish sorts out the ingredients and the steps.

Faster imports. Links LinkDish has already read come back almost instantly.

Questions

A few things
before dinner.

Is LinkDish free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 3 recipe imports and up to 15 saved recipes, along with cook mode, the shopping list and offline access. No card is required. Plus adds 100 imports a month and unlimited saved recipes for $2.99 CAD a month or $24.99 CAD a year.

Where can I use LinkDish?

LinkDish runs on Android and as a web app in any modern browser. On iPhone and iPad, open the web app in Safari and add it to your Home Screen. A native iPhone app is on the way, and you can join the list to hear when it is ready.

Which links and sources work?

Public recipe web pages, YouTube videos and Shorts with the recipe in the description, TikTok videos with the recipe in the caption, and Pinterest pins that link to a recipe page. You can also scan a photo or screenshot, or paste recipe text. Pages behind a login, Instagram and Facebook are not supported yet.

How does the meal planner work?

Add recipes from your cookbook to the days you plan to cook them. LinkDish then builds one shopping list for the whole week, grouped by aisle, with matching ingredients combined.

Can LinkDish convert between US and metric?

Yes. Switch any recipe between US and metric measurements, including oven temperatures, so 350°F becomes 175°C. You can scale servings up or down too.

Can my family share recipes and a shopping list?

Yes, with the Family plan. Up to 6 people share one cookbook and one shopping list that stays in sync for everyone.

Can I export my recipes or bring them from Paprika?

Yes. You can export a full backup of your cookbook at any time, and import recipes from a Paprika export.

Does LinkDish keep the original recipe source?

Always. Every recipe keeps its original source visible and one tap away, so the person who wrote it gets the credit.
