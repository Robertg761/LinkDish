Frequently asked

Importing recipe links.

How do I save a recipe from a website?

Copy or share the public recipe URL to LinkDish. It turns the useful recipe details into a clean cookbook entry while keeping the original source linked.

Can LinkDish import every recipe website?

Public recipe pages work best. Pages that require a login or block access may not import.

Can I organize more than fifteen recipes?

Yes. The free plan holds up to fifteen saved recipes. Plus and Family include unlimited saved recipes.
