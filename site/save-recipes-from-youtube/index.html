Good to know

What works,
and what doesn't yet.

  • Recipe in the description

    The best case. If the ingredients and method are written out under the video, LinkDish turns them into a full recipe.

  • Recipe on the creator's site

    If the description only links to a blog post, open that link and save the recipe page instead. It usually imports even more cleanly.

  • Recipe only on screen

    When the recipe is only shown in the video, take a screenshot of the ingredient list and scan it, or paste the text if you have it.

TikTok works the same way with captions: share a TikTok whose caption spells out the recipe. Pinterest pins are followed to the recipe page they link to. Instagram and Facebook links aren't supported yet.

Questions

YouTube recipe
questions.

Can LinkDish save recipes from YouTube Shorts?

Yes. Shorts, regular videos, embedded videos and live links are all read the same way, from the recipe written in the video description.

What if the video description has no recipe?

LinkDish will tell you it couldn't find a recipe. If the description links to the creator's website, save that page instead, or scan a screenshot of the recipe.

Does LinkDish credit the creator?

Yes. Every recipe saved from YouTube keeps the video as its source, one tap away, so the person who made it gets the credit.

Can I save recipes from TikTok too?

Yes, when the recipe is written in the TikTok caption. Share the TikTok to LinkDish or paste its link.
