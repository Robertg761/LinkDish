Where Paprika may still suit you better

If you want a single one-time purchase, a native iPhone or desktop app today, or your personal recipes synced across every device, Paprika does those well.

If you cook from links, videos and photos, want to try before paying, or share meals with a household, give LinkDish a try on the Free plan.

Before you switch

Questions to ask
any recipe app.

Can I import my recipes from Paprika?

Yes. Export your recipes from Paprika and import the export in LinkDish. Titles, ingredients, steps and notes come across, plus favorites and ratings when the export includes them.

Can I test LinkDish without paying?

Yes. The Free plan includes 3 recipe imports and up to 15 saved recipes, and no card is required.

Does LinkDish work on iPhone?

There is no native iPhone app yet. The LinkDish web app works in Safari and can be added to the Home Screen, and a native iPhone app is on the way.

Is LinkDish affiliated with Paprika?

No. LinkDish is an independent recipe app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Paprika or Hindsight Labs.
