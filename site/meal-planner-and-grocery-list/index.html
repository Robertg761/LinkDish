Questions

Planning
questions.

How does the LinkDish meal planner work?

Add recipes from your cookbook to the days of the week you plan to cook them. When the week looks right, LinkDish builds one shopping list for all of them.

Does the grocery list combine the same ingredient from different recipes?

Yes. Matching ingredients are combined into one line with the total amount, and the list is grouped by aisle.

Can my family share the shopping list?

Yes, with the Family plan. Up to 6 people share one cookbook and one shopping list that stays in sync for everyone.

Can I switch between US and metric amounts?

Yes. Recipes can be shown in US or metric measurements, including oven temperatures, and servings can be scaled up or down.
